Privacy Policy

Meenzy Studio app · last updated 2 November 2026

Who we are

Meenzy Studio runs the YouTube channel Tilliwink. Contact: meenzystudio@gmail.com.

The app

The Meenzy Studio app is a private tool used only by Meenzy Studio to publish its own videos to its own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Google data the app uses

With the channel owner's permission (Google sign-in), the app uses the YouTube Data API to read the basic information of our own channel and to upload and manage our own videos and their details on that channel. It does not access data about viewers, comments or other channels.

Storage and sharing

The sign-in and the information about our own videos are stored privately and securely and are never published. We do not sell or share Google user data, do not use it for advertising, and do not use it to develop, improve or train artificial-intelligence models. Data is only disclosed if the law requires it.

Meenzy Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and deleting data

Access can be removed at any time at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. To have stored data deleted, write to meenzystudio@gmail.com; we delete it within 7 days.

Children

Tilliwink publishes videos made for children. The app does not collect any information from children or other viewers.

Changes

If the app starts to use other Google data, this page is updated first and the date above changes.
